About RoxIntel

Operational expertise and analytical rigor aren’t competing values — they’re complementary forces.

Company Overview

Built on 22+ years of mission-driven analysis

RoxIntel is a federally registered Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) purpose-built to deliver mission-grade intelligence, data analytics, and precision-driven solutions to federal agencies, defense clients, and government prime contractors.

Founded by a decorated U.S. military veteran with 22+ years of military experience — spanning data analysis, operational assessment, and command-level decision support — RoxIntel was established on a simple but uncompromising principle: that operational expertise and analytical rigor are not competing values, but complementary forces.

Our Approach

Closing the gap between raw data and decisive action

Where many consulting firms offer analysis divorced from operational context, RoxIntel embeds real-world mission experience directly into its methodology — from intelligence analysis frameworks shaped by high-stakes operational environments to data visualization products designed for executive and operational decision-makers alike.

The firm supports federal clients across intelligence, defense, program management, cybersecurity, and workforce development domains, offering both strategic advisory and hands-on execution capabilities at the speed and agility only a focused small business can provide.

Just as flight-critical systems demand zero tolerance for error, RoxIntel holds its work products to the same standard of exactness, reliability, and mission readiness.
